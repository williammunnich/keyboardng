Help & keys

Space play/pause · Home rewind · ←/→ previous/next bar · Esc stop.

No MIDI keyboard? Use the computer keyboard: A S D F G H J K are white keys from C, W E T Y U black keys, Z/X change octave. You can also click the on-screen keys.

Each part can be set to Me (you play it, it is silent in the backing), Backing (the app plays it) or Off.
